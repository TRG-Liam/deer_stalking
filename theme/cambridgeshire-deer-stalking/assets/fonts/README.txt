Four font files are needed here before the theme ships (theme.json references them):
- cormorant-garamond-500.woff2
- cormorant-garamond-600.woff2
- inter-400.woff2
- inter-500.woff2
Download the latin subsets from Google Fonts (fonts are hosted locally for GDPR and performance). Until they exist, the theme falls back to Georgia and system sans, which is acceptable for local preview only.
